# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

> Find answers to frequently asked questions about Refunds via Razorpay and IRCTC refunds.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

> [accordion] How do I initiate a refund?
>
> You can issue partial or full refund using:
>
> * [The Dashboard](https://razorpay.com/docs/pos/refunds/issue.md#issue-refunds).
> * [APIs](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/refunds.md).
>
> By default, the entire amount is refunded. If you issue a partial refund, ensure the partial refund option is selected and enter the desired value in INR.
>
> > [!WARNING]
> >
> > **Watch Out!**
> >
> > Razorpay issues immediate refunds. Once a refund is issued, it cannot be canceled or reversed.

> [accordion] Do you charge for refunds?
>
> No, we do not charge for the regular refunds. However, fees and taxes charged for a captured payment are not reversed.

> [accordion] I am unable to refund a payment. What do I do?
>
> If your current balance is less than the amount you are trying to refund, you can either initiate the refund once you receive further payments or you can [add funds](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/dashboard/account-settings/balances.md#add-funds-to-reserve-balance) to your account from the Dashboard.
