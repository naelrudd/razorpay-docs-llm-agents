# Become a Technology Partner

> Submit a request to become a Razorpay Technology Partner to offer a seamless payment experience for your clients within your website/app.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

All Partners are onboarded as Service Partners by default. You can request a switch if you have a use case for the Technology Partner type. Know how to become a [Razorpay Partner](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/new-to-razorpay.md).

## Request a Switch

To request a Partner type switch:

1. Log in to the Dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Partner** → **Home**.
3. Click **Explore Now**.

   ![Initiate Partner type switch](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-type-switch-explore-now.jpg)
4. On the **Tell us more about the services you provide** page, select the relevant business type and click **Next**.

   ![Partner type switch - What services do you offer?](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-type-switch-services-offered.jpg)
5. If your business type is best suited for the Service Partner type, you will see the below screen.

   ![Partner type switch denied](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-type-switch-denied.jpg)
6. If a different Partner type is more suitable for your business, you will see the below screen.

   ![Partner type switch - evaluate business use case](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-type-switch-evaluate-usecase.jpg)
7. Select **Yes** if you have a product to manage payments for your sub-merchants and click **Next**.
8. Enter your phone number, website URL, and business details, and click **Next**.

   ![Partner type switch - fill in details](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-type-switch-business-details.jpg)

You have successfully submitted a request for a Partner type switch. Our team will reach out to you with further instructions.

## Next Steps

If your switch request is approved, you will have to complete [full KYC](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/technology-partners/kyc.md) to start receiving commissions.

> [!WARNING]
>
> **Watch Out!**
>
> Filling out the form does not guarantee a Partner type switch. Our team will evaluate your use case and contact you for more information. This process can take around 2 to 3 weeks.
