# Third-Party Service Providers

> Steps to download reports from Linked Account Dashboard and the list of available reports.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

Third-party service providers can download reports from their Linked Account Dashboards and reconcile transactions.

## Get Dashboard Access

To download reports, the Platform must provide Dashboard access to your Linked Accounts. To provide Dashboard access:

1. Log in to the Dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Route** under **Products**.

   ![Partners Dashboard select Route](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-dashboard-route.jpg)
3. In the **Accounts** tab, enable **Dashboard Access** for the required **Account Id**.

   ![Enable Razorpay Dashboard access for Technology Partners](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/enable-dashboard-access-platform.jpg)
4. Once the Dashboard access is granted, a password reset link is sent to the Linked Account's registered email id.
5. Reset the password and log in to the Linked Account Dashboard.

## Download Reports

Watch this video to see how to generate the Linked Account Transfers Report.

[YouTube video player](https://www.youtube.com/embed/UgqBLfX4xBM)

To download reports:

1. Log in to the Dashboard.
2. In the left menu, navigate to **Reports**.

   ![Partners Dashboard Reports](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/reports-partners-dashboard.jpg)
3. Select the report type, period and format.
4. Click **Generate Report**.
5. It can take a few minutes to a couple of hours to generate a report depending on the data size. Once the report is ready, you can download it from the **Recent Reports** tab.

## Available Reports

Below is the list of reports available to you.

| Report | Description |
| - | - |
| Transfers Report | List of transactions collecting Platform fees from your clients. |
| Settlements Report | List of all Settlements made to your bank account. Settlement refers to amount credited to your account. |
| Settlements Recon Report | List of Settlements made to your bank account and the associated transfers. You can use this to reconcile your settlements with your platform fees. |
| Combined Report | List of all debits and credits of your account. |
| Reversals Report | List of platform fee reversals made from a Linked Account. |
