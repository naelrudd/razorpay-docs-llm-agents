# Become a Razorpay Partner

> Sign up as a Razorpay Partner, whether you are new to Razorpay or already a merchant.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

How you sign up depends on whether you already have a Razorpay account.

> [card] [New to Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/new-to-razorpay.md)
>
> Sign up as a Partner and activate your account by completing KYC.

> [card] [Existing Razorpay Merchant](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/existing-merchant.md)
>
> Add a Partner account from the Partners webpage or your Razorpay Dashboard.
