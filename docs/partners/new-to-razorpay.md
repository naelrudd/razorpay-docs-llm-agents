### Sign Up and KYC

> [accordion] Sign up on  Partners Page
>
> If you are new to Razorpay, you must first sign up for a Razorpay account.
>
> Follow these steps:
>
> 1. Navigate to the [Partners](https://razorpay.com/partners) webpage and click **Become a Partner** or **Sign Up**.
>
> > [accordion] Sign up using email ID
> >
> > 2. Enter your email id and click **Continue**.
> > 3. Enter a password and click **Create Password**.
> >
> >    ![partners - enter password](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-onboarding-new-password.jpg)
> > 4. Enter the OTP sent to your registered email and click **Verify**.
> > 5. Enter your name and optionally, your number. Click **Continue**.
> >
> >    ![partners - enter name](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-onboarding-new-name.jpg)
> >
> > This completes the account creation process.
>
> > [accordion] Sign up using phone number
> >
> > 2. Enter your phone number and click **Continue**.
> > 3. Enter the OTP sent to your registered phone number and click **Verify**.
> >
> >    ![partners - enter otp](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-onboarding-new-otp.jpg)
> > 4. Enter your name and optionally, your email. Click **Continue**.
> >
> > This completes the account creation process.

> [accordion] Complete KYC on  Dashboard
>
> As a Razorpay Partner, you are eligible to receive [commissions](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/commissions) from Razorpay. You must complete the account activation and KYC process to receive the commission. We recommend you perform this step using a desktop computer.
>
> To activate your account:
>
> 1. Log in to the Dashboard and navigate to **Partner** → **Home**.
> 2. Click **Submit KYC**.
>
>    ![Complete Partner KYC on Razorpay Dashboard](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-dashboard-submit-kyc.jpg)
> 3. In the **CONTACT DETAILS** section, fill in your name, email and number. Click **Save & Next**.
>
>    ![Partners KYC Contact Details](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-kyc-contact-details.jpg)
> 4. In the **BUSINESS DETAILS** section, select your **Business Type** and fill relevant details. Click **Save & Next**.
>
>    ![Partners KYC Business Details](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-kyc-business-details.jpg)
> 5. Fill the **ADDRESS DETAILS** section. Click **Save**.
>
>    ![Partners KYC Address Details](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-kyc-address-details.jpg)
> 6. Read and agree to the **Razorpay Terms and Conditions**. Click **Submit and Verify**.
>
> After you submit the details, our team will review the KYC form.
>
> * We will notify you via email, SMS and WhatsApp if we need further information.
> * The review process takes 4-5 days.
> * Upon successful verification:
>   * Razorpay displays the **Settlements Enabled** success message on the Dashboard.
>   * Razorpay deposits the settlement amount in your bank account per your settlement schedule.

## Commissions

Commissions on your account are enabled instantly after successful completion of KYC. Know how the [commission is calculated](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/commissions). In case of any queries, contact our [Partnerships team](mailto:partnerships@razorpay.com).
