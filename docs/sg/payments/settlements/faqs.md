## Settlements

> [accordion] What are settlements?
>
> Settlement is the process in which the money received from your customers is settled in your bank account. Settlements for all payments will be done in SGD (Singaporean Dollars), irrespective of the currency in which the payment was made by the customer. Settlement cycle is subject to bank approval and can vary based on your business vertical, risk factor, and so on. Each settlement generated has a unique settlement id attached to it along with the amount settled. Know more about [Settlements](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/settlements).

> [accordion] What is the settlement cycle you offer?
>
> Our standard settlement cycle is T+2 working days, T being the date of transaction capture. This means that the captured payments are settled within 2 working days from the date of capture.

> [accordion] The status of my settlement shows as failed on the Dashboard. What do I do?
>
> Check if you have received a email from our Support Team. Please revert to the mail with the necessary details. If you have not received any email from Razorpay, please contact our [Support Team](https://razorpay.com/sg/support/) for assistance.

> [accordion] How to reconcile settlements along with the transactions made?
>
> You can [download a daily or a monthly report](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/magic-checkout/analytics/reports.md#generate-reports) for the **Settlement Reconciliation Report** from the **Reports** section on the Razorpay  Dashboard. The report contains transactions and the corresponding settlement ids.
