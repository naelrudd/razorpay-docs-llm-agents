# About International Payments

> Accept payments in international currencies, through international bank accounts and/or payments via cards issued by foreign banks. Check the list of supported purpose codes and currencies.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

You can accept payments from your customers using our products, such as Payment Gateway, Payment Links and Subscriptions. Razorpay supports [3D Secure 2.0](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/payment-methods/cards/features/3ds2.0.md) for international cards.

### Singaporean Businesses

Singaporean businesses can easily activate international payment methods to expand their global reach.

**Foreign-issued cards:** Singapore businesses can collect payments from foreign-issued cards in 160+ currencies across all payment products, including Payment Gateway, Payment Links and Subscriptions.

To enable international payments, please raise a request with the Razorpay [Support team](https://razorpay.com/sg/support/).

**Indian payment methods:** Singaporean businesses can accept payments from Indian customers using local payment methods such as UPI, netbanking, credit cards and debit cards. There is no need for additional paperwork or registration. The payments collected will be directly settled into the business's overseas bank account.
