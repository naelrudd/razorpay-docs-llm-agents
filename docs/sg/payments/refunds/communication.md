# Refund Communication

> Check the refund-related emails sent to your customers after the refunds are initiated.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

If a customer is asking for a refund, the banking partner provides a unique reference number when a refund is processed. The customer can use this reference number to track the refund status with the bank.

As a customer, you will be notified of the specific payment to be refunded in 7-10 working days.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Customer Looking for Refund**
>
> If you are a customer looking for a refund, know more about [customer refunds](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/customers/customer-refunds.md).

## Refund Mailers

Razorpay sends you the following email communications for refunds:

**Refund Mailer**\
&#x20;After you process the refund, Razorpay sends you an email with the refund id and unique reference number provided by the bank.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Handy Tips**
>
> Razorpay sends the **Refund** emailers only if the customer email is passed to us.
