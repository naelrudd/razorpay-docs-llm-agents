# Ecommerce

Manage all your payments and financial operations from a consolidated Dashboard. Create an online store, track payments, and issue refunds within minutes. Make better business decisions with real-time reporting. Following are the use cases and recommended products.

## Handle End-to-End Payments

Experience the future of payments with the easiest integration, online onboarding and feature-filled checkout. Avail of the lowest industry charges. Following are the recommended products:

> [tab] Payment Gateway
>
> * One-Click Retry on Razorpay Checkout.
> * Integrate with a wide coverage of SDKs and plugins for all popular marketplaces such as Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento and more.
> * Enjoy high success rates on Card payments.
> * Access payment methods including, Cards (debit and credit) and PayNow.
>
> Know more about [Payment Gateway](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/payment-gateway).

> [tab] Payment Links
>
> * Share the Payment Link via an email, SMS or messenger and get paid immediately.
> * Recover abandoned carts by sending customers a Payment Link, encouraging them to return and complete their purchase.
>
> Know more about [Payment Links](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/payment-links).

> [tab] Payment Button
>
> * Add a pay button to your website to accept one-time and subscription payments on your website in less than 5 minutes.
> * Share automated payment receipts with your customers.
>
> Know more about [Payment Button](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-button).

## Go Global

Businesses tend to find accepting international payments quite cumbersome due to issues such as currency conversion, FIRS collection from banks and the constant threat of credit card fraud. Following is the recommended product:

> [tab] Address Verification System
>
> * Confirm that the card user is the rightful owner.
> * Verify the cardholder’s billing address and card details.
>
> Know more about [Address Verification System](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/international-payments/address-verification-system.md).

## Automate Recurring Revenues

With Razorpay, you can offer easy and tailor-made subscription plans to your customers through a platform built for automation. We recommend:

> [tab] Subscriptions
>
> * Offer customers various Subscription plans, such as monthly, quarterly, and yearly options.
> * Securely store card information, eliminating the need for customers to enter card details every time they make a recurring payment.
> * Automate the entire payment process, charging customers' cards at predefined intervals based on their chosen Subscription frequency.
> * Provide discounts or cashback to increase average order value, thereby boosting sales.
>
> Know more about [Subscriptions](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/subscriptions)
