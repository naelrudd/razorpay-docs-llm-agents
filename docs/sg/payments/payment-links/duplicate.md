# Duplicate a Payment Link

> Duplicate a Payment Link from the Razorpay Dashboard.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

You can duplicate an existing Payment Link from the Dashboard. This saves you time and effort when creating multiple links to be sent to different customers. You can duplicate links in any state. Know more about [Payment Link states](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/payment-links/states.md).

## Duplicate a Payment Link From Dashboard

To duplicate a Payment Link:

1. Log in to the Dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Payment Links**.
3. Click on the Payment Link you want to duplicate. The details appear on the right panel.
4. Click the duplicate icon.
5. Edit the required fields on the **Create Payment Link** screen. For example, customer details, receipt number and notes.
6. Click **Create Payment Link**.
