# Standard Payment Links

> Create Standard Payment Links and share them with your customers through SMS and emails.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

You can send the Standard Payment Links as URLs to customers through SMS and emails. Customers can open these URLs and make payments using cards, PayNow and wallet payment methods.

## Payment Link States

Know about the different [Standard Payment Link states](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/payment-links/states.md#standard-payment-links).

## Create

Create a Standard Payment Link from the [Dashboard](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/payment-links/create.md#create-a-standard-payment-link-from-dashboard) or using our [APIs](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/api/payments/payment-links/create-standard.md).
