# About Dashboard

> Overview of the Razorpay Dashboard home page with key features including payments overview, insights, and product recommendations for managing your payment operations.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

The Dashboard Home page serves as your central command center for managing payment operations on Razorpay. It provides a comprehensive overview of your account status, recent payment activity, key insights and personalised product recommendations to help you optimise your payment infrastructure.

The Dashboard offers a wide range of features, such as:

* **Analytics** and **real-time charts** that provide valuable insight into your business performance.
* List of **recent activities** that may require your action.
* **Configure, operate, and manage** your Payment Gateway (PG) account and other Razorpay products.
* An overview of recent **payments, settlements and refunds**.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Handy Tips**
>
> You must have a Razorpay account to access the Dashboard. Do not have a Razorpay account? [Set up a Razorpay account](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/set-up).

## Key Section of Payments Dashboard Home Page

The following information is shown on the Payments Dashboard Home page.

> [accordion] Account Balance and Payment Information
>
> Your current account balance is displayed on the Dashboard. Payment Overview data provides an overview of the total collected amount in you current balance, total  number of **Refunds** made and the total number of Orders created by your customers for the selected time range.

> [accordion] Payment Information
>
> Payment Volume data provides an overview of the Payment Volume and Number of Payments received for the selected date range.

> [accordion] Analytics
>
> You can view detailed information about your account activities. This includes a wide range of analytics and real-time charts that provide insight into the performance of your business and help you take informed actions if necessary.
>
> You can view the following information:
>
> * Successful and failed payment count.
> * Successful and failed refund count.
> * Filter data to display it on a Daily, Weekly or Monthly basis.
> * Group data by Payment Methods.
> * Hover over the pie diagram to view the amount split for each payment method.
> * **Payment method split** data displays the distribution of various payment methods for collected payments for the selected duration.

> [accordion] Recent Activity
>
> This section displays recent Payment related activities. Click the respective payment id for more information about the transaction.
