# Accept Payments from International Customers

> Razorpay offers affordable solutions for Indian businesses to accept International Bank Transfers and International Card Payments, with funds settled directly to local bank accounts, ensuring minimal conversion loss and a smooth global payment experience.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

Razorpay provides Singaporean businesses with a comprehensive suite of payment solutions to accept international payments effortlessly. Our platform supports various payment methods, allowing businesses to cater to global customers securely and efficiently.

## Supported Payment Methods

> [accordion] International Cards
>
> Singaporean businesses can accept payments from international debit and credit cards issued across the globe. Our platform ensures a seamless experience for both businesses and customers.
>
> > [accordion] Use Cases / Challenges
> >
> > * **Ecommerce:** Accept payments from global customers for products or services.
> > * **Education:** Collect tuition fees from international students.
> > * **Challenges:** Exchange rate fluctuations, compliance with international regulations, and varying card acceptance rates.
>
> > [accordion] Prerequisites
> >
> > To accept international payments, you need to meet the eligibility criteria established by our banking partners. Know more about the [prerequisites](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/international-payments/international-debit-credit-cards.md#eligibility-and-prerequisites).
>
> > [accordion] Onboarding Flow
> >
> > To enable international payments, please raise a request with the Razorpay [Support team](https://razorpay.com/sg/support/).
>
> > [accordion] Method Details
> >
> > * **Card Types:** Visa, MasterCard, American Express and UnionPay.
> > * **Currencies:** Accept payments in multiple currencies, ensuring convenience for international customers.
> > * **Security:** PCI DSS compliance and robust fraud detection.
