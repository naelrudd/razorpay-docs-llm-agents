# Set up a Razorpay Account

> Set up a Razorpay account, submit your KYC using CKYC/Video KYC and start using the various Razorpay Payments and Banking Plus products.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

Sign up for a Razorpay account to start accepting customer payments using our products.

> [card] 1. Sign Up
>
> [Set up an account](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/set-up#1-sign-up) to use Razorpay products to accept customer payments. \
> You can also [set up multiple accounts](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/set-up#set-up-multiple-accounts) with the same mobile number and email id.

> [card] [2. Submit KYC](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/set-up#2-submit-kyc-details)
>
> Submit your KYC documents to get your account verified and get the payments settled in your account.

> [!TIP]
>
> **Assisted Onboarding**
>
> Hire a dedicated onboarding manager for an additional fee to assist you through your onboarding journey. The onboarding manager will provide end-to-end assistance, from sign-up and KYC document submission to verification and account activation. Know more about [Assisted Onboarding](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/set-up#assisted-onboarding).

## 1. Sign Up

To set up a Razorpay Account, go to the [Razorpay website](https://razorpay.com/) and click **Sign Up**. Follow these steps for a smooth sign up process:

> [accordion] Contact Details
>
> Provide your contact details to get started:
>
> 1. Enter your **Mobile Number**.
> 2. Click **Send OTP**.
> 3. Enter the **OTP** sent to your mobile number. If you did not receive the OTP, click **Resend OTP**.
> 4. Click **Submit OTP**.
> 5. Enter your **Name** and click **Continue**.

> [accordion] Business Details
>
> Provide the following business details:
>
> 1. Enter your **Brand Name**. This should be the name of your business that your customers recognise.
> 2. Click **Continue**.

> [accordion] Communication Details
>
> Provide details to receive account updates:
>
> 1. Enter your **email id** to receive account updates on your email and click **Send OTP**.
> 2. Enter the **OTP** sent to your email id and click **Continue**.
>
> You can start accepting payments from customers once your KYC is completed.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **After Your Account is Created**
>
> * You can accept payments using a [range of products after KYC completion](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments.md).
> * You can access the **Test** mode on the Dashboard to test and experience the products offered by Razorpay. Test mode does not involve actual money transactions.
> * You have to generate separate API keys for Live and Test modes. Know more about [API Keys](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/api/authentication.md#generate-api-keys).

## 2. Submit KYC Details

Upon creating an account, you can start accepting payments from customers once your KYC is completed. The operations team will subsequently contact you to activate your account.

**After Successful Activation**

* Accept payments without any amount limits.
* Razorpay will automatically settle payments in your bank account as per your [settlement cycle](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/settlements#settlement-cycle).
* Request for [other payment methods](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-methods#view-payment-methods).

## Set Up Multiple Accounts

As a business owner, you may need different accounts for different kinds of work.
However, it is challenging to obtain a new mobile number each time you want to set up an account. With Multi-Account, you can set up different accounts using the same mobile number and email id as your existing account.

#### Example

Assume Alex is a Singaporean business owner who has an account with Razorpay using Mobile Number: `+6591119111` and Email Id: `alex.lim@example.com`.

Alex uses **Multi-Account** feature to set up another account. Razorpay  will use the same number and email id as shown above to set up the other account. This will work in a similar way in your respective country.

> [accordion] Set up Another Account Using Website
>
> 1. Go to the [website](https://razorpay.com/) and click **Sign Up**.
> 2. Enter your mobile number or email id and click **Continue**.
> 3. Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number and click **Verify**.
> 4. All the accounts linked to the entered number/email is are displayed. Click **Create a new account**.
>
>    ![Create another account](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/create-multi-account.jpg)
> 5. Select the country and click **Verify**.
> 6. Your existing number and email id is already registered with the new account. Enter your name and continue with [Sign Up](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/set-up#1-sign-up) and [KYC](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/set-up#2-submit-kyc-details).

> [accordion] Set Up Another Account Using Dashboard
>
> 1. Log in to the Dashboard.
> 2. Click the profile icon → **Switch Merchant**.
> 3. Click **Create A New Account**.
>
>    ![Create multi account](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/create-new-account-switch-merchant.jpg)
> 4. Select the country and click **Verify**.
> 5. Your existing number and email id is already registered with the new account. Enter your name and continue with [Sign Up](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/set-up#1-sign-up) and [KYC](https://razorpay.com/docs/sg/payments/set-up#2-submit-kyc-details).

> [!WARNING]
>
> **Watch Out!**
>
> If your mobile number is already linked to multiple user accounts with different email ids, you cannot use the **Multi-Account** feature to set up multiple accounts using that number. In this case, use another mobile number or sign up using your email id.
>
> * Account 1: Mobile Number: `+6581118111` | Email Id: `alex.lim@example.com`
> * Account 2: Mobile Number: `+6581118111` | Email Id: `alex.lim+99@example.com`
>
> If Alex tries to use the **Multi-Account** feature to make another account, Razorpay will block the creation of a new account. This will work similarly in your respective country.

## Assisted Onboarding

> [accordion] Why Hire an Onboarding Manager
>
> * **Dedicated onboarding manager**: Receive personalised support with a dedicated onboarding manager assigned to you within **1 hour**.
> * **Swift account activation**: Get your account activated within 8 business hours and start accepting payments via Razorpay.
> * **End-to-end assistance**: Receive comprehensive support until you complete your first transaction, addressing any queries throughout the process.

> [accordion] How to Hire an Onboarding Manager
>
> 1. On the [Razorpay sign-up page](https://razorpay.com/), sign up using your mobile number.
> 2. Click **Know more** under **Hire an onboarding manager for faster account set up!**. Complete the payment.
>
>    ![Hire a Razorpay onboarding manager.](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/hire-onboarding-manager.jpg)
>
> An onboarding manager will be assigned to you within 1 hour to help you set up your account.
