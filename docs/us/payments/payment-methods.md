# About Payment Methods

> Check the different payment methods that can be configured to accept payments from your customers using Razorpay products.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

The Razorpay Checkout offers Card and ACH Direct Debit payment methods.

## Supported Payment Methods

Following are all the payment modes that the customer can use to complete the payment on the Checkout.

| Payment Method | Code | Availability |
| - | - | - |
| [Debit and Credit Cards](https://razorpay.com/docs/us/payments/payment-methods/cards.md) (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Diners Club & Discover) | `card` | ✓ |
| [ACH Direct Debit](https://razorpay.com/docs/us/payments/payment-methods/ach.md) | `ach` | Requires Approval and Integration. |

We are soon looking to support more methods including Apple Pay, PayPal amongst many other international payment methods.

## Payment Method Error Codes

There are certain error codes specific for each payment method supported by Razorpay. Know more about the [Payment Method Error Codes](https://razorpay.com/docs/us/errors/payments/payment-methods-error-parameters.md).
