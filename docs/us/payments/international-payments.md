# About International Payments

> Accept payments in international currencies, through international bank accounts and/or payments via cards issued by foreign banks. Check the list of supported purpose codes and currencies.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

Accept payments from customers using foreign-issued cards through our products, such as Payment Gateway and Payment Links. Razorpay supports [3D Secure 2.0](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-methods/cards/features/3ds2.0.md) for international cards.

### American Businesses

American businesses can easily activate international payment methods to expand their global reach. \


**Foreign-issued cards:** American businesses can collect payments from foreign-issued cards in local American currency across all payment products, including Payment Gateways and Payment Links.

To enable international payments, please raise a request with the Razorpay [Support team](https://razorpay.com/us/support/) or contact your dedicated sales POC.

**Indian payment methods:** American businesses can accept payments from Indian customers using payment methods such as credit cards and debit cards. There is no need for additional paperwork or registration. The payments collected will be directly settled into the business's overseas bank account.
