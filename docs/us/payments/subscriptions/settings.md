# Subscriptions Settings

> Configure payment method settings for Razorpay Subscriptions.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

You can configure the card payment method to accept subscription payments from your customers.

## Enable Payment Methods

To enable payment methods:

1. Log in to the Dashboard and click **Subscriptions** under **PAYMENT PRODUCTS** in the left menu.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Enable the card payment method.
