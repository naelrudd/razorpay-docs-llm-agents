# Supported Payment Methods

> Learn about the payment methods supported by Razorpay Subscriptions, including Cards, UPI Autopay and Emandate.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

Razorpay Subscriptions supports recurring payments via cards. Customers enter their card details to authorise the subscription. The payment is then debited automatically based on the selected plan.
