# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

> Find answers to frequently asked questions about RazorpayX Account Types.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

> [accordion] What is a Business Account?
>
> Your RazorpayX account is referred to as a business account. This account has an account number and an IFSC. You can transfer funds to and from this account, store them and make payouts from it, as and when you wish.

> [accordion] How do I get my Business account?
>
> Your business account is created when you [set up your RazorpayX account](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/set-up.md). You can start transacting after account activation.

> [accordion] Can I have more than one Business account?
>
> You can open [RazorpayX powered Current Account(s)](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/account-types/current-account.md) as business account(s) under a single Merchant ID.

> [accordion] Can I share my business account details with my business contacts to receive funds?
>
> Yes, you can share your business account number and IFSC with your business contacts to receive funds. They will be able to add your business account number to their bank portals and transfer funds to your account.

> [accordion] How can I view all transactions against my business account?
>
> You can view all transactions made against your business account via your RazorpayX Dashboard under [Account Statement](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/account-statement). You can also fetch all your transactions using [APIs](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/x/transactions/fetch-all.md).

> [accordion] How can I check my current balance against my Business account?
>
> Your current balance is visible to you on the Dashboard as soon as you log in.

> [accordion] Will I have access to all my merchant accounts on the RazorpayX platform?
>
> Yes. Just like on your Razorpay Dashboard, you can [switch between different accounts on your RazorpayX Dashboard](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/dashboard), provided you have been added as a team member on your other RazorpayX accounts.
