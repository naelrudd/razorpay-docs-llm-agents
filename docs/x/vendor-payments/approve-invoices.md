# Approve Invoices

> Approve or reject invoices via email with RazorpayX.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

You can approve or reject invoices directly through email, without logging into the RazorpayX Dashboard.

## Invoice Approval Email

You can approve or reject the invoice attached in the email. Click **Approve** or **Reject** as required.

![Approval email](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/x-vp-approval-email.jpg)
