# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

> Find answers to frequently asked questions about RazorpayX Contacts.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

> [accordion] What is a Contact in RazorpayX?
>
> A Contact is an entity to whom payouts can be made through supported modes such as UPI/IMPS/NEFT/RTGS.

> [accordion] How do I create a Contact?
>
> You can create Contacts on RazorpayX by providing the Contact name, email id, mobile, and so on. Contacts can be created via:
>
> * [API](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/x/contacts/create.md)
> * [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/contacts)
> * [Bulk Upload](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/contacts/bulk-uploads.md)
>
> Know how to [create a Contact](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/contacts#create-a-contact).

> [accordion] Can I classify a Contact?
>
> Yes, you can classify a Contact and assign a type or a category to that Contact. For example, Contact types can be `Vendor`, `Employee` or other custom types as applicable to your business.
>
> Know more about [Contact types](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/contacts#contact-types).

> [accordion] What is meant by '**Active**' and '**Inactive**' contact?
>
> When you create a Contact, it is in the **Active** status by default.
>
> However, for business needs, you can [mark a Contact as '**Inactive**'](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/contacts#mark-contact-inactive). If marked '**Inactive**', you cannot [create Payout](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/payouts#how-to-make-payouts) to that Contact.
>
> If you want to resume payouts to a Contact, mark them as 'Active' again.

> [accordion] What modifications can I make to a Contact once created?
>
> Once created, you can modify any property of the Contact. In fact, you can also mark a Contact as '**Inactive**' or '**Active**'. Know more about [Contact actions](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/contacts).

> [accordion] How do I block payouts to a Contact?
>
> You can [mark a Contact as '**Inactive**'](https://razorpay.com/docs/x/contacts#mark-contact-inactive) to block any payout requests to a Contact.

> [accordion] How do I resume payouts to a blocked Contact?
>
> Mark the Contact as ‘Active’ to resume payouts to the Contact.

> [accordion] Can I automatically import my Linked Accounts and Customers from the Razorpay Payments Dashboard to RazorpayX Dashboard?
>
> No. You cannot automatically import Customers and Linked Accounts from Razorpay Payments Dashboard to RazorpayX Dashboard.
