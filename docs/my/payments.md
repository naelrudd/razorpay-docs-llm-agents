# Razorpay Curlec Payments Suite

> Explore Razorpay Curlec Payment Gateway and the entire suite of products available as part of Razorpay Curlec Payments Suite.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

Razorpay Curlec enables businesses to accept, process, and disburse payments seamlessly across multiple channels. Whether you are running an online store, managing subscriptions or facilitating marketplace transactions, Razorpay Curlec provides the tools and infrastructure to handle payments at scale.

> [card] [Accept Payments](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments.md#accept-payments)
>
> Set up payment gateways, process transactions and manage customer payments across multiple channels.

> [card] [Disburse Payments](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments.md#disburse-payments)
>
> Send bulk payouts, vendor payments and instant transfers with automated reconciliation.

> [card] [Solutions by Industry](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments.md#solutions-by-industry)
>
> Find tailored payment solutions for ecommerce, SaaS, marketplaces and enterprise businesses.

> [card] [Solutions by Platform](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments.md#solutions-by-platform)
>
> Integrate with e-commerce plugins, SDKs for mobile and web and comprehensive APIs for custom builds.

### Disburse Payments

Use Razorpay Curlec products to split and transfer payments collected from customers.

#### Route

![Route - Split and Transfer Payments](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/route-product.jpg)

Split incoming payments automatically to multiple parties with [Razorpay Curlec Route](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/route). Perfect for marketplaces, platforms and franchises.
