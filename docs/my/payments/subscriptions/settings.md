# Subscriptions Settings

> Configure payment method settings for Razorpay Curlec Subscriptions.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

You can configure payment methods such as Cards and Wallets (Touch'n Go) using which you would like to accept subscription payments from your customers.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Handy Tips**
>
> Cards and Wallets (Touch'n Go) currently support recurring payments up to RM 25,000.

## Enable Payment Methods

To enable payment methods:

1. Log in to the Dashboard and click **Subscriptions** under **PAYMENT PRODUCTS** in the left menu.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Enable the payment methods.

   <ul>
     <li>**Card**: Enable this to accept recurring payments via cards for your Subscriptions.</li>

     <li>**Touch'n Go**: Enable this to accept payments through the Touch'n Go wallet when a recurring charge is less than RM 25,000.</li>
   </ul>
