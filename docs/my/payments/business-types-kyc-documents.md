# KYC Documents for Companies and Businesses

> List of required KYC Documents for companies and business types to register with Razorpay Curlec under the 2026 Master KYC guidelines.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇺🇸 United States

Below are the business types supported by Razorpay Curlec and the KYC documents required for various businesses.

## Business Types

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Handy Tips**
>
> * All businesses are required to provide the first page of their latest bank statement.
> * In case you wish to use bank statement of your personal account, ask your Razorpay Curlec POC for an authorisation letter/form. You will be required to fill your details on the letter and return it on your company's letterhead.
> * We currently do not support Individual/Unregistered businesses.

> [accordion] Razorpay Curlec supports the following businesses:
>
> | Business Type | Description | Example |
> | - | - | - |
> | LLP | LLP stands for Limited Liability Partnership. It is a combination of partnership and corporation. The partners have limited liability, which means that the partners' personal assets are not used for paying off the company's debts. Know about the [documents](#document-requirements) you need to submit for Razorpay Curlec's KYC verification. | Dental offices, auditing firms, financial advising services, business consultancies and so on. |
> | Partnership | A partnership is an arrangement between two or more people who agree to be the co-owners, distribute responsibilities for running an organisation and share the income or losses the business generates. Know about the [documents](#document-requirements) you need to submit for Razorpay Curlec's KYC verification. | Law firms, physician groups, real estate investment firms and so on. |
> | Private Limited | A private limited company is a business entity held by a small number of people. It does not offer stocks for sale to the general public. Know about the [documents](#document-requirements) you need to submit for Razorpay Curlec's KYC verification. | Syahlam Sdn Bhd, Adabi Consumer Industries Sdn Bhd, Aegis Malinnov Sdn Bhd and so on. |
> | Public Limited | A public limited company is a business entity that has limited liability and offers shares to the general public. Know about the [documents](#document-requirements) you need to submit for Razorpay Curlec's KYC verification. | Maybank Bhd., Axiata Group Bhd., Hong Leong Financial Group Bhd. and so on. |
> | Sole Proprietorship | This business entity is owned, controlled and operated by a single individual who is the sole beneficiary of all profits or losses and responsible for all risks. Know about the [documents](#document-requirements) you need to submit for Razorpay Curlec's KYC verification. | Small businesses such as a local grocery store, a local clothes store, an artist, freelance writer, IT consultant, freelance graphic designer and so on. |
> | Trust | A trust is a [fiduciary relationship](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/glossary.md) in which a [trustor](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/glossary.md) gives the [trustee](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/glossary.md) the right to hold title to property or assets for the benefit of a third party, the [beneficiary](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/glossary.md). Trusts are established to provide legal protection for the trustor’s assets. Know about the [documents](#document-requirements) you need to submit for Razorpay Curlec's KYC verification. | A beneficiary may transfer property to a trustee and grant them the right to manage it. |

## Document Requirements

| Business Type | Required Documents |
| - | - |
| **Private/Public Limited** | SSM |
| **Sole Proprietorship** | SSM |
| **Partnership** | SSM |
| **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)** | SSM, LLP Certificate |
| **Societies/Clubs/Associations/Joint Management Bodies** | <ul><li>Certificate of Registration</li><li>Appointment Letter for Chairman, Treasurer & Secretary **OR** Circular Resolution/Latest Minutes of Meeting (to prove that Chairman, Treasurer & Secretary are the attendees)</li><li>Organization Chart</li><li>ROS Certificate</li></ul> |
| **Professional Services** | SSM and License from government boards, bureaus, divisions or offices |

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Handy Tips**
>
> Razorpay Curlec will download the **SSM**. You do not need to submit it unless we are unable to retrieve it from the **CTOS system**.

## Additional Documents

Depending on the line of business, we may also ask for additional certifications.

> [accordion] List of additional certificates depending on your business category
>
> | Business Type | Required Documents |
> | - | - |
> | Travel, Tourism & Hotel Related | MOTAC License: KPK/LN or Sijil Pendaftaran sebagai Pengendali Premis Penginapan from Jabatan Kastam Diraja Malaysia |
> | Pharmaceutical Related | MOH License/Lab Test |
> | Insurance Agencies | Reseller or Appointment Letter |
> | Legal Services | Bar Council Document/Registration |
> | Doctor | Practitioner Certificate |
> | Veterinarian | Practitioner Certificate |
> | Pharmacy | MOH or Pharmacy License |
> | Education & Training Related | Trainer Certificate (**Case-by-case basis**) |
> | Medical and Health Care Services | MOH-related Certificate |
> | Financial Services | BNM/SC Registration or License |
> | Direct Selling | AJL License/Proof of Membership with DSAM |
> | Transportation (Car Rental & Vehicle Leasing) | MOT License |
> | Government-Sanctioned Lottery/Betting | License from Government |
> | Pawn Shop | Akta Pemegang Pajak Gadai (Jadual C) |
> | Essential Needs Items (Mask, Sanitizer, Thermometer, etc.) | Official Distributor Letter or Lab Test Report for Brand Owner |
> | Alcohol Business | Alcohol License (Public or Beer House License) |
> | Vape Products | MOH License |
