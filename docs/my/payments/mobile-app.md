# About Razorpay Curlec Payments Mobile App

> Use the Razorpay Curlec Payments Mobile App to track payments and settlements, issue refunds and accept payments from customers by sharing Payment Links and Razorpay Curlec.me URL.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia

Razorpay Curlec Mobile App helps you to keep track customer payments even when you are on-the-move. This app enables you to:

* View real-time status on payments made by customers.
* Accept payments from your customers using Payment Links.
* Issue refunds to customers.
* View and track settlement details.
* Generate API keys.

> [!WARNING]
>
> **Watch Out!**
>
> Razorpay Curlec utilises the app name - **Razorpay Curlec Mobile App** across all supported geographies.

## Advantages

* Payment and settlement data now at your fingertips.
* Failed payment? No problem! Create and share a Payment Link.
* Issue refunds in a few taps.

## How it Works

Follow these steps:

1. Install the Razorpay Curlec Mobile App on your [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.razorpay.payments.app\&utm_campaign=mobile%20app%20with%20PL\&utm_medium=email&_hsmi=2&_hsenc=p2ANqtz--g-8t5duramBL4wnhFg5y5-Bb1sPtleTBTuK8zwBIxtS4LqXxcds_9fEskknybVZfsEvGaAdG2WRsheyOfgBSC4oWUskNoyqI1A4dbomcURuKzc0g\&utm_content=2\&utm_source=hs_email) smartphone.
2. Log in to the Dashboard with your credentials. If you do not have an account, [create a new Razorpay Curlec account](https://curlec.com/onboarding/my/?).
3. You can now perform any of the following operations:
   * Accept payments from your customers using [Payment Links](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/mobile-app/accept-payments/payment-links.md).
   * [View status of payments](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/mobile-app/track-manage.md) made by customers.
   * [Issue partial or full refund](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/mobile-app/track-manage/issue-refunds.md) to customers. You can issue instant or regular refunds.
   * [View and track settlements](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/mobile-app/track-manage/view-settlements.md).
   * [Generate API keys](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/mobile-app/accept-payments/payment-gateway.md#steps-to-generate-api-keys).
