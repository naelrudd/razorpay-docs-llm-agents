Razorpay Curlec provides comprehensive payment solutions that solve unique challenges across the lending, wealth management and insurance sectors. Following is the use case and recommended products.

## Manage Collections

Payment failures in financial services can result in significant revenue loss, with over 50% of customers dropping off if payments fail on the first attempt. Our suite of intelligent features is built to address this and provide best-in-class success rates. Following are the recommended products:

> [tab] Payment Gateway
>
> * Integrate with a wide coverage of SDKs and plugins for popular marketplaces like Shopify, WooCommerce, and more.
>
> * Access payment methods including, Cards (debit and credit), Wallets and FPX.
>
> * Quickly recoup 15% of your failed payments on the first attempt with seamless and intuitive retry.
>
> Know more about [Payment Gateway](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-gateway).

> [tab] Payment Links
>
> * Share Payment Links via email, SMS, or messenger and get paid immediately.
>
> * Recover abandoned carts by sending customers a Payment Link, encouraging them to complete their purchase.
>
> Know more about [Payment Links](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-links).

## Automate Recurring Revenues

Recurring revenues are core to financial services, including insurance premiums, SIPs and loan repayments. With Razorpay Curlec, you can offer easy and tailor-made subscription plans to your customers through a platform built for automation. Following are the recommended products:

> [tab] Subscriptions
>
> * Set up mandates, authorising the bank to deduct loan instalments automatically from their bank accounts at predefined intervals.
> * Provide flexible repayment schedules based on customer preferences and loan terms.
> * Automate the reconciliation of loan repayments, provide real-time visibility into payment statuses and reduce the chances of errors in financial reporting.
>
> Know more about [Subscriptions](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/subscriptions).
