# Business Website Details

> Steps to add or update your business website or app details on the Razorpay Curlec Dashboard. You can also add or update additional website details.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

You must provide your live website or mobile app details during account activation.

* Without these details, you will not be able to access live mode API keys and hence **cannot accept payments from your website or app**.
* You can still accept payments from customers using  **Payment Links** generated via the Dashboard.

## Add and Edit Website/App/Additional Websites

> [tab] Add Website/Mobile App
>
> To add your website or mobile app details:
>
> 1. Log in to the Dashboard.
> 2. Click **Account & Settings** in the left navigation.
> 3. Click **Business website detail** under **Website and app settings** section.
> 4. Click **Add website/app details**.
> 5. Click **Proceed to update website/app**.
> 6. Ensure you have the following pages- **Shipping policy**, **Contact us**, **Pricing details**, **Terms and conditions**, **Privacy policy** and **Cancellation and Refunds policy**.
> 7. Select the relevant answers and add the link for your website/app. \\

> [tab] Update Website/Mobile App
>
> To update website or mobile app details:
>
> 1. Log in to the Dashboard.
>
> 2. Click **Account & Settings** in the left navigation.
>
> 3. Click **Business website detail** under **Website and app settings** section.
>
> 4. Click **Edit** next to the website/app that you want to edit.
>
> 5. Click **Proceed to update website/app**.
>
> 6. To update your **Website** or **App**:
>
> > [accordion] Follow these steps if you own a website:
> >
> > 1. Select **Website**.
> > 2. Enter the **Website URL**.
> > 3. Provide the links for these pages: **About us**, **Contact us**, **Pricing details**, **Terms and conditions**, **Privacy policy** and **Refunds policy**.
> > 4. Upload a sample invoice. The accepted file formats are PNG, JPG and PDF.
> > 5. Provide your website's **Test Account credentials** if required or select **No** for **Does your website require login from user to complete payment?** if logging in is not required.
> > 6. Click **Submit website for review**.
> >
> >    ![Enter Website URL](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/update-website.jpg)
> >
> > After the details are provided, our team will review the request. We will update the details on the Dashboard.
>
> > [accordion] Follow these steps if you own an app:
> >
> > 1. Select **App**.
> > 2. Enter the **App URL**.
> > 3. Provide your website's **Test Account credentials** if required or select **No** for **Does your app require login from user to complete payment?** if logging in is not required.
> > 4. Click **Submit app for review**.
> >
> >    ![Enter App URL](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/update-app.jpg)
> >
> > After the details are provided, our team will review the request. We will update the details on the Dashboard.
>
> > [!NOTE]
> >
> > **Handy Tips**
> >
> > We may reach out to you for clarifications via email, WhatsApp, SMS and Dashboard during the verification process.
> > Navigate to **Account & Settings** and submit the necessary information in the appropriate section. Our team will go through the information you provide and help you resolve the issue.

> [tab] Additional Website/Mobile App
>
> You can accept payments using your Razorpay Curlec account on more than one website. To do this, you need to share with us the details of the additional web pages.
>
> > [!NOTE]
> >
> > **Handy Tips**
> >
> > This feature is available only if your services/products fall under the e-commerce business category.
>
> To add additional website:
>
> 1. Log in to the Dashboard.
>
> 2. Click **Account & Settings** in the left navigation.
>
> 3. Click **Business website detail** under **Website and app settings** section.
>
> 4. Click the plus icon next to the **Additional Business Website/App** field.
>
>    ![Add additional business website or app](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/dashboard-guide-myaccount-profile-update-website.jpg)
>
> 5. Click **Proceed to update website/app**.
>
>    ![Proceed to update website or app](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/dashboard-guide-myaccount-profile-additional-website.jpg)
>
> 6. Provide the website or app details:
>
> > [accordion] Follow these steps if you own a website:
> >
> > <ol><li>Select **Website**.</li>
> > <li>Enter the **Website URL**. </li>
> > <li>Provide the links for these pages: **About us**, **Contact us**, **Pricing details**, **Terms and conditions**, **Privacy policy** and **Cancellation/Refund policy**.</li> \
> > &#x20;![Enter additional website URL and details](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/dashboard-guide-myaccount-profile-additional-website-links.jpg)
> > <li>**Upload invoice**: Upload a sample invoice. The accepted file formats are PNG, JPG and PDF.</li>
> > <li>**Reason for adding new website**: Enter the reason for adding a new website. Enter a minimum of 50 words.</li>
> > <li>Provide your website's **Test Account credentials** if required or select **No** for **Does your website require login from user to complete payment?** if logging in is not required.</li>
> > <li>Click **Submit website for review**.</li></ol>
> >
> > ![Upload invoice and submit reason for adding new website](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/dashboard-guide-myaccount-profile-additional-website-submit.jpg)
>
> > [accordion] Follow these steps if you own an app:
> >
> > 1. Select **App**.
> > 2. Enter the **App URL**.
> > 3. **Reason for adding new website**: Enter the reason for adding a new website. Enter a minimum of 50 words.
> > 4. Provide your website's **Test Account credentials** if required or select **No** for **Does your app require login from user to complete payment?** if logging in is not required.
> > 5. Click **Submit app for review**.
> >
> >    ![Enter app URL and submit reason for adding new app](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/dashboard-guide-myaccount-profile-additional-app.jpg)
> >
> > After you provide the details, our team will review the request. We will update the details on the Dashboard.
> >
> > ![Additional website added or updated is under review](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/dashboard-guide-myaccount-profile-additional-app-pending.jpg)

* If you add or update your **primary website**, instant checks are run to validate your website, policy pages and business industry. In case the website requirements are not met, you can re-check and enter the valid URLs as prompted or create policy pages with Razorpay Curlec.

* If you add or update **additional websites**, our team will review manually and notify you.
