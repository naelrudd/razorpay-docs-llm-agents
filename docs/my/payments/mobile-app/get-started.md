# Get Started

> Create a Razorpay Curlec account and generate API keys using Razorpay Curlec Payments Mobile App.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia

If you are new to Razorpay Curlec, you can create an account right from the mobile app. If you are an existing Razorpay Curlec user, log in to the Razorpay Curlec  Mobile app using your Dashboard credentials.

Know how to:

* [Create a New Account](#create-a-new-account).
* [Sign in with an existing Razorpay Curlec account](#sign-in).

## Create a New Account

Follow these steps to create a Razorpay Curlec account:

1. Install the Razorpay Curlec Mobile App from Google Play Store.

2. Open the app and tap **Create Account**.

3. Enter your mobile number or email address. Tap **Next**.

4. Create a password and tap **Create Account**.

5. A verification code is sent to your **mobile number** or **email id**. Enter the code to verify the mobile number or email id.

6. **Business Overview**: Enter your business details, billing label and provide your website/app URL. If you do not have one, select the relevant option. Tap **Save & Next**.

7. **Business Details**: Enter details such as business PAN and name, authorised signatory's PAN and name and the business address. Tap **Save & Next**.

8. **Contact Details**: Enter your name and contact details. Enable the **Get account updates on WhatsApp** option to receive WhatsApp notifications. You can [disable WhatsApp notifications](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/dashboard/account-settings/email-sms-whatsapp.md) from the Dashboard. Tap **Submit**.

9. Your account is activated.

   ![Account activated on the Razorpay Curlec payments mobile app](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/payments-mobile-app-activate.jpg)

Live mode is enabled once you complete the activation process.

> [accordion] Submit KYC Details
>
> Submit KYC details for a Razorpay Curlec account on the mobile app. Tap **Complete KYC** to initiate the account activation process.
>
> Enter the following details:
>
> 1. **Bank and Company Details**: Enter the bank account details to which Razorpay Curlec should transfer the amount collected from customers (minus fees and tax).
> 2. Enter your **Business Registration Number** issued by SSM. If you do not have the registration number, select **My Business is not registered with SSM** check box and provide the **Certificate of Registration/Incorporation**.
> 3. Tap **Save and Verify**.
>
> Our team will review the information submitted by you. It takes approximately 3 working days to complete the review process.
>
