# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

> Find answers to frequently asked questions about Razorpay Curlec Invoices.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia

> [accordion] I have raised an invoice. How do I make changes to it?
>
> You can make any changes to an invoice in `draft` status. Know more about [updating an invoice](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/invoices/update.md).
>
> > [!NOTE]
> >
> > **Handy Tips**
> >
> > If an invoice is issued to a customer, you can make only limited changes such as Expiry Date, Customer Comments and Terms and Conditions. If you need to make other changes to an `issued` invoice, [cancel the invoice](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/invoices/cancel.md) and [create a new invoice](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/invoices/create.md) with the revised details. However, you can cancel an **unpaid** issued invoice.

> [accordion] I want to set a date by which the customer must make the payment. How do I do that?
>
> You can set Expiry Date for the invoices. The customer should make a payment against the invoice within this Expiry Date. The customer cannot make payments for an expired invoice. Know more about [setting Expiry Date for an invoice](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/invoices/create.md#create-an-invoice-from-dashboard).

> [accordion] What is the difference between Issue Date and Expiry Date?
>
> Refer to the [Glossary](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/invoices/glossary.md) for information about the difference between Issue Date and Expiry Date.

> [accordion] What are the various payment methods available to the customer?
>
> Below are the available payment methods:
>
> * Cards
> * FPX
> * Wallets

> [accordion] Where do I track the invoices and their status?
>
> You get instant notifications about the invoices. You can also subscribe to webhook events and generate reports. Know more about [tracking invoices and viewing reports](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/invoices/how-it-works.md#step-4-track-invoices-and-reports).

> [accordion] The customer wants a hard copy of the invoice. Where do they get it?
>
> Your customers can download a PDF copy of the invoice sent to them via email. Open the invoice and click **Download PDF**.

> [accordion] Can I add more than one billing address for a customer?
>
> You can add a maximum of three billing addresses for one customer id.

> [accordion] Can I use Razorpay Curlec to automatically generate invoices/receipts for customers after they complete payment on my mobile app or website?
>
> No, [Razorpay Curlec Invoices](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/invoices) is designed to initiate and collect payments, not to generate accounting invoices or receipts after payment completion.
