# About Card Payments

> Accept payments from your customers using cards.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

You can accept payments from your customers using debit or credit cards from all international providers. You can explore our [3D Secure 2.0 Authentication Protocol (3DS2)](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-methods/cards/features/3ds2.0.md) feature.

## Supported Card Networks

Razorpay Curlec supports the following card networks:

* Visa
* Mastercard

## Related Features

* [Instalments](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-methods/instalments): let your customers split a card purchase into fixed monthly payments.
* [Flash Checkout](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/dashboard/account-settings/checkout-features.md#flash-checkout): let your customers securely save their card details for faster transactions.
