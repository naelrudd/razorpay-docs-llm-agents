# About Wallets

> Accept payments using various Wallets like Touch'n Go and GrabPay at Razorpay Curlec Checkout.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia

With Razorpay Curlec, you can offer your customers Wallets as a payment method. A digital wallet is like a virtual version of your physical wallet. It stores your money and payment methods electronically on your device. You can use it to make quick and secure payments online or in stores, providing convenience and an extra layer of security compared to traditional methods.

## Supported Wallets

The table below lists the various wallets available to you.

| Wallet Provider | Wallet Code | Availability |
| - | - | - |
| [Touch 'n Go](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-methods/wallets/touch-n-go.md) | `touchngo` | ✓ |
| [Boost](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-methods/wallets/boost.md) | `boost` | ✓ |
| [MCash](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-methods/wallets/mcash.md) | `mcash` | ✓ |
| GrabPay | `grabpay` | ✓ |
| [Alipay+](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-methods/wallets/alipay-plus.md) | — | ✓ |

[Alipay+](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-methods/wallets/alipay-plus.md) gives your customers a single integration to seven overseas wallets: GCash (Philippines), DANA (Indonesia), TrueMoney (Thailand), Alipay (China), AlipayHK (Hong Kong), Kakao Pay (South Korea) and Rabbit LINE Pay (Thailand). The wallets above are for customers paying in Malaysia; Alipay+ is for overseas customers.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Additional Payment Methods**
>
> You can view the wallets enabled for your Razorpay Curlec account from the **Account & Settings** → **Payment methods** tab, and raise a request with our [Support team](https://curlec.com/support/) for additional wallets.
