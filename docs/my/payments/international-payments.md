# About International Payments

> Accept payments in international currencies, through international bank accounts and/or payments via cards issued by foreign banks. Check the list of supported purpose codes and currencies.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

Accept payments from customers using foreign-issued cards through our products, such as Payment Gateway, Payment Pages, Payment Buttons, Payment Links and Invoices. Razorpay Curlec supports 3D Secure 2.0 for international cards.

### Malaysian Businesses

Malaysian businesses can easily activate international payment methods to expand their global reach. \


**Foreign-issued cards:** Malaysian businesses can collect payments from foreign-issued cards in local Malaysian currency across all payment products, including Payment Gateways, Payment Pages, Payment Buttons, Payment Links, Invoices and Routes.

To enable international payments, please raise a request with the Razorpay Curlec [Support team](https://curlec.com/support/).

**Indian payment methods:** Malaysian businesses can accept payments from Indian customers using local payment methods such as [UPI](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-methods/upi.md), credit cards and debit cards. There is no need for additional paperwork or registration. The payments collected will be directly settled into the business's overseas bank account.
