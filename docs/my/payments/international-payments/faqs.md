# International Payments | FAQs

> Find answers to frequently asked questions about Razorpay Curlec International Payments.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

> [accordion] Can NGOs accept international payments via ?
>
> Due to certain compliance guidelines, we cannot provide the international payment feature to NGOs.

> [accordion] Do you support companies registered outside Malaysia?
>
> Yes, Razorpay Curlec serves businesses registered in Malaysia, India, Singapore and United States.

> [accordion] Do you support wire transfers for international payments?
>
> No. Currently, we only support international payments made using cards issued by overseas banks for all the major networks, following are the supported card networks:
>
> * Visa
> * Mastercard

> [accordion] Is there a list of  products that support international payments?
>
> Yes. Know more about the [products that support](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/international-payments#supported-products) international payments.

> [accordion] What is the settlement cycle for international payments?
>
> The default settlement cycle is as per applicable law(s). You can view your settlement cycle on the Razorpay Curlec Dashboard.

> [accordion] How does the settlement happen for international payments?
>
> The Settlement currency is MYR (Malaysian Ringgits) for all transactions made using Razorpay Curlec. Thus, international payments are settled in MYR.
