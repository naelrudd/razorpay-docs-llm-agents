# International Debit and Credit Cards

> Enable/disable international payments using foreign cards. Check the eligibility criteria, prerequisites and the application process.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

Accept international payments from foreign bank cards by raising a request with the Razorpay Curlec [Support team](https://curlec.com/support/).

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Feature Enablement**
>
> International payments require special security and risk checks. We enable this feature only after our banking partners have approved it, ensuring compliance and protecting against fraud.

## Enable Payments from International Debit and Credit Cards

To enable international payments for the first time, raise a request with the Razorpay Curlec [Support team](https://curlec.com/support/).

> [accordion] Eligibility and Prerequisites
>
> You must meet the following eligibility criteria set by our banking partners to accept international payments:
>
> * You must have an active Razorpay Curlec account with KYC verification completed.
> * You must have a valid website with the following sections/pages clearly defined:
>   * **Terms and Conditions**
>
>   * **Privacy policy**
>
>   * **Refund and Cancellation policy**
>
>   * **Shipping policy**
>
> > [!WARNING]
> >
> > **Watch Out!**
> >
> > International payments cannot be enabled for your account without these sections/pages on your website.
>
> * Check the list for the [business categories not supported by Razorpay Curlec](https://curlec.com/s/terms-of-use/).

> [accordion] Application Process
>
> To onboard and accept payments from international customers, please raise a request with the Razorpay Curlec [Support team](https://curlec.com/support/).
