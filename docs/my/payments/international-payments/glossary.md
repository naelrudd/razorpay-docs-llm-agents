# Glossary

> List of commonly used terms related to Razorpay Curlec International Payments.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

The following table lists all the commonly used terms in international payments and their descriptions.

| Terms | Description |
| - | - |
| International Payments | International payments are transactions where the payer and the payee are in different countries, involving cross-border funds transfer and currency conversion processes. |
| Currency Conversion​ | The process of converting one currency into another during an international transaction. Razorpay Curlec Settlements occur in SGDMYRUSD based on the conversion rate at the time of payment. |
| Non-3DS Transactions | Payments processed without the 3D Secure protocol, often used for international card transactions. These transactions carry higher risk due to the lack of an additional authentication step. |
