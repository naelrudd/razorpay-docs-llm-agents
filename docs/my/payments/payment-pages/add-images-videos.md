# Add Images, Videos and More

> Add images, videos, hyperlinks, create lists and change font style and colour for your Razorpay Curlec Payment Pages.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Handy Tips**
>
> If you are using a template, the sample description only contains heading levels and content.

You can add the following to your Payment Pages:

> [accordion] Add Headings
>
> You can define up to 2 heading levels. Select **Heading** for your major level 1 headings and **Subheading** for level 2 heading and **Normal Text** for content.
>
> > [!NOTE]
> >
> > **Handy Tips**
> >
> > Defining heading levels in your description makes the information look more structured and neat.
>
> ![Add Heading to a Payment Page.](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/pp-addheading.jpg)

> [accordion] Change Font Style and Color
>
> Choose a font color from 10 available colors. There are three font styles that you can apply to the text: boldface, italics and underline

> [accordion] Create Lists
>
> You can create lists to display the information in a sequence. You can create bulleted lists and numbered lists.

> [accordion] Add an Image
>
> Click the image icon to upload an image from your local system. We support images with dimensions 40x40. Formats supported are JPG, JPEG, PNG and GIF.

> [accordion] Add a Video
>
> Click the video icon and **Enter Video URL**. This will embed the hosted video into your page.
>
> ![Add Videos to a Payment Page](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/pp-add-video.jpg)

> [accordion] Add a Hyperlink
>
> URLs entered as plain-text in the Description field are automatically converted to hyperlinks. You can use the **Link** icon to add YouTube or Vimeo video links to text. You cannot add links to images.

> [accordion] Add Sharing Options
>
> The **+Add social media share icons** option adds CTA (call-to-action) buttons for Facebook, X and WhatsApp.

> [accordion] Add Contact Information
>
> Click **Add your contact information** to add contact details. You can add phone number and email address.

> [accordion] Add Notes
>
> Click **Add Terms & Conditions** to add the terms and conditions for a transaction.
