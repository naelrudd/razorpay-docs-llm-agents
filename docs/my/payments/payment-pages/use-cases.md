# Payment Pages Use Cases

> Explore the diverse use cases of Razorpay Curlec Payment Pages, a robust payment collection solution for businesses.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

Use Razorpay Curlec Payment Pages, an ideal solution for businesses and organisations to manage their transactions efficiently and securely across diverse industries. With Payment Pages, you can accept payments for various purposes, including event tickets, donations, and educational fees.

> [accordion] Events and Tickets
>
> Acme Conferences implements Razorpay Curlec Payment Pages to streamline ticket sales for a range of events, including professional conferences and exhibitions.
>
> Benefits for Acme Conferences:
>
> > [accordion] Customisable Payment Pages
> >
> > Create branded, event-specific Payment Pages that can handle various ticketing options and add-ons.
>
> > [accordion] Seamless User Experience
> >
> > Offer attendees a straightforward purchasing path from page visit to payment completion.
>
> > [accordion] Timely Updates
> >
> > Keep track of sales with real-time notifications.
