# Shopify Razorpay Secure Plugin Prerequisites

> Know how to integrate Razorpay Curlec Payment Gateway with your Shopify store. Complete guide for Shopify payment integration with Razorpay Secure app.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

Razorpay Shopify integration allows you to accept payments on your Shopify store using the Razorpay Secure Payment Gateway app.

> [card] [Troubleshooting & FAQs](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-gateway/ecommerce-plugins/shopify-razorpay-secure/faqs.md)
>
> Troubleshoot common error scenarios and find answers to frequently asked questions about the Razorpay Shopify integration.

**Before you proceed:**

* Sign up for a [Razorpay Curlec account](https://accounts.curlec.com/auth/).
* Sign up for a [Shopify account](https://www.shopify.in).
* [Ensure that your website](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/payment-gateway/ecommerce-plugins/shopify-razorpay-secure/faqs.md#i-am-getting-the-following-error-message-email-id-mismatch-why) is reviewed before initiating integration with Shopify.
* A Razorpay Curlec Account with access to the account owner’s credentials. (Only owners can connect Razorpay Curlec with Shopify, and API keys are not required for integration.)

> [!WARNING]
>
> **Watch Out!**
>
> Only Razorpay Curlec account owners can connect their Shopify account with Razorpay Curlec.
