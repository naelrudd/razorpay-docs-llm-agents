# Set up a Razorpay Curlec Account

> Set up a Razorpay Curlec account, submit your KYC using CKYC/Video KYC and start using the various Razorpay Curlec Payments and Banking Plus products.

Available in
🇮🇳 India
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇺🇸 United States

Sign up for a Razorpay Curlec account to start accepting customer payments using our products.

## Sign Up

To set up a Razorpay Curlec account, go to the [Razorpay Curlec website](https://curlec.com/) and click **Sign Up**. Follow these steps for a smooth sign up process:

> [accordion] Basic Details
>
> Provide the following details to get started:
>
> 1. Enter your **phone number** or **email** and click **Continue**.
>
>    ![curlec onboarding](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/curlec-onboarding-new.jpg)
> 2. Enter OTP and click **Verify**.
>
>    ![curlec enter otp](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/curlec-enter-otp-new-1.jpg)
> 3. Enter your **Name** and click **Next**.
> 4. Enter your **Email** id and click **Next**.
>
>    ![enter email for curlec](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/curlec-email.jpg)
> 5. **Enter OTP** and click **Next**.
> 6. Enter your **Business Name**. This name must be the same as mentioned in your legal documents.
>
> You can now explore the Razorpay Curlec Dashboard in Test Mode. However, to start accepting payments, you must complete onboarding.

> [accordion] Business Details
>
> Provide the following business details:
>
> 7. Enter your **Business Registration Number** issued by SSM. If you do not have the registration number, you can select **My Business is not registered with SSM** check box and provide the **Certificate of Registration**/**Incorporation**.
>
>    ![Razorpay Curlec business registration number](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/curlec-registered-business-name.jpg)
> 8. Select whether you business is **Registered** or **Unregistered**. If your business is registered, select the business type from:
>    * Proprietorship
>    * Partnership
>    * Private Limited (Sdn Bhd)
>    * Public Limited (Bhd)
>    * LLP
>    * Trust
>    * Society
> 9. Select your **Business Category** and click **Next**.
>
>    ![curlec- select business category](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/curlec-business-category.jpg)
> 10. Select a **Sub Category** and click **Next**.
> 11. Select how you want to accept payments. In case you choose **Others**, explain how you will accept payments and click **Next**.
>
> ![accept payments via curlec](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/curlec-accept-payments.jpg)
>
> 12. Enter you Business **Address** and **Postcode**. Same as operational address check box is selected by default, in case the address is different, de-select and enter the operational address.

> [accordion] Bank Details
>
> Provide the following details for transactions:
>
> 13. **Select Bank** linked to your registered business, from the drop-down list.
> 14. Enter the **Bank Account Number** and **Account Holder's Name**. Click **Next**.
>
> ![add bank details](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/curlec-bank-details.jpg)

> [accordion] Document Upload
>
> Provide the documents in PDF format.
>
> 15. Based on the chosen business type, a list of documents are asked for KYC. Select the document, upload the required file and click **Save**. Know more about the required KYC documents.
> 16. Once all the documents have been uploaded, click **Next**.
>
> ![upload documents for curlec KYC](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/curlec-document-upload.jpg)

**After Your Account is Created**

* Accept payments upto RM 30,000.
* You can explore the [range of products](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments.md).
* You can access the **Test** mode on the Dashboard to test and experience the products offered by Razorpay Curlec. Test mode does not involve actual money transactions.
* You have to generate separate API keys for Live and Test modes. Know more about [API Keys](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/api/authentication.md#generate-api-keys).

## Set Up Multiple Accounts

As a business owner, you may need different accounts for different kinds of work.
However, it is challenging to obtain a new mobile number each time you want to set up an account. With Multi-Account, you can set up different accounts using the same mobile number and email id as your existing account.

#### Example

Assume Nur is a Malaysian business owner who has an account with Razorpay Curlec using Mobile Number: `+60123456789` and Email Id: `nur.aisyah@example.com`.

Nur uses **Multi-Account** feature to set up another account. Razorpay Curlec  will use the same number and email id as shown above to set up the other account. This will work in a similar way in your respective country.

> [accordion] Set up Another Account Using Website
>
> 1. Go to the [website](https://curlec.com/) and click **Sign Up**.
> 2. Enter your mobile number or email id and click **Continue**.
> 3. Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number and click **Verify**.
> 4. All the accounts linked to the entered number/email is are displayed. Click **Create a new account**.
>
>    ![Create another account](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/create-multi-account.jpg)
> 5. Select the country and click **Verify**.
> 6. Your existing number and email id is already registered with the new account. Enter your name and continue with [Sign Up](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/set-up#sign-up) and [KYC](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/set-up#business-details).

> [accordion] Set Up Another Account Using Dashboard
>
> 1. Log in to the Dashboard.
> 2. Click the profile icon → **Switch Merchant**.
> 3. Click **Create A New Account**.
>
>    ![Create multi account](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/create-new-account-switch-merchant.jpg)
> 4. Select the country and click **Verify**.
> 5. Your existing number and email id is already registered with the new account. Enter your name and continue with [Sign Up](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/set-up#sign-up) and [KYC](https://razorpay.com/docs/my/payments/set-up#business-details).

> [!WARNING]
>
> **Watch Out!**
>
> If your mobile number is already linked to multiple user accounts with different email ids, you cannot use the **Multi-Account** feature to set up multiple accounts using that number. In this case, use another mobile number or sign up using your email id.
>
> * Account 1: Mobile Number: `+60123456780` | Email Id: `nur.aisyah@example.com`
> * Account 2: Mobile Number: `+60123456780` | Email Id: `nur.aisyah+99@example.com`
>
> If Nur tries to use the **Multi-Account** feature to make another account, Razorpay Curlec will block the creation of a new account. This will work similarly in your respective country.
