# Video Tutorials

> Explore various tutorials for employee-facing features on the Payroll Dashboard.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

Explore video tutorials for the features available to the employees of an organisation on the Payroll Dashboard.

* Expand the relevant accordion to view the video tutorial.
* Click the documentation links within the accordion to view the detailed textual guide.
