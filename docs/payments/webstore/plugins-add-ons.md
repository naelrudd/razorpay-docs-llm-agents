# Track Payments Using Google Tracking ID and Facebook Pixel

> Add your Google Tracking ID and Facebook Pixel to your Razorpay Webstore to track payments and conversions from your Google and Facebook advertisements.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

If you use Razorpay Webstore to accept payments from customers and also:

* Advertise on Google and use Google Tracking ID to track conversions
* Advertise on Facebook and use Facebook Pixel to track conversions

You can do the following to track Google and Facebook ads and analyse conversions:

* [Add Google Analytics Tracking ID to Webstore](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/webstore/plugins-add-ons/google-analytics.md)
* [Add Facebook Pixel to Webstore](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/webstore/plugins-add-ons/fb-pixel.md)
* [Add Facebook Pixel to your payment success page](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/webstore/plugins-add-ons/fb-payment-success.md)
