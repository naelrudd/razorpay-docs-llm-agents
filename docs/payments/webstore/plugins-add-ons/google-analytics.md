# Add Google Analytics Tracking ID

> Add your Google Tracking ID to your Razorpay Webstore to track payments and conversions from your Google advertisements.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

You can add your Google Tracking ID to your Webstore to track payments and conversions from your Google advertisements. Know more about [Tracking ID](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1008080?hl=en) and how to create one using Google Analytics account.

**Available Tracking Metrics**

| Metric | Description |
| - | - |
| Page View | When a customer lands on your Webstore. This is the default metric to track page visits. |

## Prerequisites

[Create a Google Tracking ID](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1008080?hl=en).

## Add Google Tracking ID to Your Webstore

To add Google Tracking ID to your Webstore:

1. Navigate to your Webstore in the edit mode.
2. Click **Page Settings**.
3. Go to **Plugins and Add-ons** and click **Configure**.

   ![Configure Plugins and Add Ons](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/pp-plugins-addons-configure.jpg)
4. Add the **Tracking ID** to track the page views.

   ![Add Google Tracking ID and FB Pixel](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/pp-plugins-addons-GA.jpg)
5. Click **Save**.
