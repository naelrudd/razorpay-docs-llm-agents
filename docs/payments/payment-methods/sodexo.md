# Sodexo

> Accept payments using Sodexo meal cards for food and beverage orders.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

Accept payments using Sodexo meal cards for food and beverage purchases with Razorpay Optimizer. By incorporating Sodexo meal card payments, businesses can offer customers a seamless and familiar payment experience, expanding the range of payment options available for food and beverage purchases.

![sodexo checkout](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/sodexo-checkout.jpg)

## Add Sodexo as a Payment Method

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Handy Tips**
>
> Sodexo integration is supported on all checkout types: [standard](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-gateway/web-integration/standard.md), [custom](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-gateway/web-integration/custom), and [server-to-server (S2S)](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-gateway/s2s-integration.md).

Follow the steps given below on the Razorpay Dashboard:

> [accordion] Step 1: Enable Optimizer and Add PayU as Payment Provider
>
> 1. Enable [Optimizer](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/optimizer) on your Razorpay account.
> 2. Log in to the Dashboard and navigate to **Optimizer**.
> 3. [Add PayU as a payment provider](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/optimizer/payu-instant.md#add-payu-as-a-payment-provider).
> 4. Select `card` and `sodexo` payment methods and submit.
>
>    ![Add Salt Key sodexo](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/add-key-salt-new-payu-sodexo.jpg)

> [accordion] Step 2: Configure Sodexo as a Payment Method
>
> 1. On the Razorpay Dashboard, navigate to **Account & Settings**.
> 2. Select **Sodexo** from the list of payment methods and click **Request**. Know more about [how to request for a payment method](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/dashboard/account-settings/payment-methods.md#request-payment-methods).
>
>    ![sodexo request](https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/sodexo-request.jpg)
>
> After the completion of the above steps, Sodexo appears on the Razorpay Checkout.
