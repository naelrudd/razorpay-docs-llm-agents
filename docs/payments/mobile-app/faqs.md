## Payment Links

> [accordion] What is a Payment Link?
>
> Payment Links are URLs that you can send to your customers through SMS and email. The customers can click on these links to make payments.

> [accordion] What payment modes does Payment Links support?
>
> Payment Links supports the following payment modes:
>
> * Debit/Credit card
> * Netbanking
> * UPI
> * Wallet

> [accordion] Can I share the Payment Links using  other apps?
>
> Yes, you can share the Payment Links using Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, SMS or email.

## App Data

> [accordion] Does this app display data from the Test mode on Dashboard?
>
> No, this app displays data only from the Live mode.

## Razorpay.Me

### For Business

> [accordion] Can I get a Razorpay.me URL if my Razorpay account is not activated?
>
> No, the Razorpay.me URL is instantly created by us after your account is activated on the mobile app. You will be unable to access it before activation.

> [accordion] Can I enter the amount that I want to receive from my customers?
>
> Yes, you can specify the amount you want to receive from your customers or they can enter an amount of their choice.

> [accordion] Can I create multiple Razorpay.me URLs?
>
> No, you can have only one Razorpay.me URL for your Razorpay account. You can use Payment Links to have multiple links and share those with customers.

> [accordion] Can I modify my Razorpay.me URL?
>
> You can [edit the Razorpay.me URL](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/mobile-app/accept-payments/razorpay-me/manage.md) immediately after account activation only. We recommend you to use the business/brand name that you are identified with by your customers.

> [accordion] Can I make my Razorpay.me URL expire after a while?
>
> Razorpay.me is a permanent URL and will not expire. Also, you cannot deactivate the URL.

> [accordion] Which report can I refer to view payments received through my Razorpay.me URL?
>
> You can view the payments on the [Track Payments](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/mobile-app/track-manage.md) page of the App.

### For Customers

> [accordion] How do I request a refund after I have made a payment through a business's Razorpay.me URL?
>
> Refer to this article to know how to [request refunds](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/customers/customer-refunds.md).

> [accordion] Can I make multiple payments to a business's Razorpay.me URL?
>
> Yes, you can make multiple payments to a business' Razorpay.me URL.
