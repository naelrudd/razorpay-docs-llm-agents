# Industry Comparison

> See how your success rate, failure rate and refund rate compare with businesses like yours, right on the Analytics Overview page.

Industry comparison shows how your success rate, failure rate and refund rate (the percentage of transactions refunded) compare with businesses like yours. See whether a number is normal for your industry and track how you stay within that range over time, all on the [Analytics Overview](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/dashboard/analytics-suite/overview.md) page.

Log in to the [Razorpay Dashboard](https://dashboard.razorpay.com/), go to **Analytics** and click **Overview**. In the filters at the top of the page, open **Compare with** and select **Industry**.

![Analytics Overview page with the Compare with filter open and Industry selected, and the Industry range shown beside the key metrics](https://mintcdn.com/razorpay-881012b3/lduAlum148c_aWjV/static/docs-home/assets/industry-comparison-select-industry.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=lduAlum148c_aWjV\&q=85\&s=6c97b04b25d47868820bb68305cc1f3c)

> [!NOTE]
>
> Industry comparison is available on request. To enable it for your account, reach out to your Razorpay Account Manager or Razorpay Tech Support.

## Compare with Your Industry

Once Industry comparison is enabled for your account, **Industry** is selected by default under **Compare with** when you open the Overview page.

With **Industry** selected, you see an Industry range next to your own numbers in three places:

* **Key metrics**: your Success rate, Failure rate and Refund rate, each with the Industry range beside it.
* **Success rate trend**: a shaded band behind your trend line.
* **Transaction failures**: an Industry range column on the source summary and on Top failure reasons.

Every number shown next to peer data is labelled **Industry**.

## Understand the Industry Range

The Industry range shows the 25th to 75th percentile of businesses like yours. In tight spaces it is written as **P25-P75**.

Use the range to read your own number:

* **Inside the range**: your number is normal for your industry.
* **Success rate below the range**, or **failure rate or refund rate above it**: worth a closer look.
* **Success rate above the range**, or **failure rate or refund rate below it**: you are doing better than most businesses like yours.

## View the Industry Range on Key Metrics

The **Key metrics** section shows your **Success rate**, **Failure rate** and **Refund rate** along with the Industry range, for example *77% vs 65-81% (Industry)*.

* Select one payment method to see the range for that method.
* Select more than one payment method to see a range blended across the methods you selected.
* Leave **Payment methods** on **All** to see an overall range.

> [!NOTE]
>
> The comparison is worked out for each payment method. For the most precise read, select one payment method and a longer period such as the last 30 days.

## Track Your Success Rate Against the Industry

The **Success rate** chart shows a shaded band labelled **Industry SR range (P25-P75)** behind your own trend line. Use it to see whether your success rate stayed inside the typical range over the period you selected.

* If you select more than one payment method, the chart shows one band for each method.
* Use the date range and the grouping options above the chart to see the trend over a different period.

## Understand Failures with the Industry Range

The **Transaction failures** section already shows why payments failed. The Industry range column shows whether a failure source or reason is unusual for your industry.

* On the **Source** summary, each source (Customer, Business, Gateway, Razorpay and Others) shows the Industry range for its share of failures.
* On **Top failure reasons**, each combination of reason, source and step shows its own Industry range.

![Transaction failures section with an Industry Range column on the Source summary and on the Top failure reasons table](https://mintcdn.com/razorpay-881012b3/lduAlum148c_aWjV/static/docs-home/assets/industry-comparison-transaction-failures.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=lduAlum148c_aWjV\&q=85\&s=c60c795375ff07403643ad475b45887d)

> [!NOTE]
>
> Hover over the **Industry range** column heading to see *25th-75th percentile among your Industry peers*.

## How Your Industry Peers Are Chosen

Your peers are other businesses with:

* The same merchant category (MCC).
* The same subcategory.
* A similar average order value, within 20% of yours.

## When Industry Comparison Does Not Show

| What you see | What it means |
| :- | :- |
| **Not enough data for an Industry comparison. Try a longer time period or a different payment method.** | We have kept a minimum bar on the number of payments needed to show Industry analytics, so the range you see is reliable. There may not be enough payments in your selected filters yet. Try a longer date range or a different payment method. |
| **Industry** is greyed out under **Compare with** | We cannot build a reliable benchmark for your business yet, for example because there are not enough businesses like yours or not enough recent payments. Changing your filters does not change this. |
| The Industry ranges are hidden | **Compare with** is set to **Previous period** or **Custom period**. Select **Industry** again to bring them back. |

> [!NOTE]
>
> If **Industry** does not appear under **Compare with** at all, Industry comparison is not enabled for your account. Reach out to your Razorpay Account Manager or Razorpay Tech Support.
