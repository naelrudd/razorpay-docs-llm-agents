# Business Categories

> Know about the business categories and subcategories and the MCC each subcategory maps to.

Available in
🇮🇳 India

Send the business category at `business_profile.business_category.category` and its subcategory at `business_profile.business_category.subcategory`. Razorpay derives the Merchant Category Code (MCC) from the subcategory. Use this page to show the categories and subcategories in your own onboarding flow.

Some subcategories ask business model questions. Know more about [business model questions](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/partners/accounts/business-model-questions.md).
